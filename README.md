## 🛠️ Advanced Prompt Engineering Frameworks

### 1. RACE Framework (Role, Action, Context, Expectation)

```markdown
[ROLE]
You are a [expert title] with [X years] experience in [domain].
Your expertise includes [specific skills/knowledge].

[ACTION]
[Specific task to perform]

[CONTEXT]
Background information:
- [Relevant detail 1]
- [Relevant detail 2]
- [Constraints or requirements]
Current situation: [Describe current state]
Goal: [Desired outcome]

[EXPECTATION]
Provide:
1. [Expected output format]
2. [Specific elements to include]
3. [Quality standards]
4. [Additional deliverables]

Output format: [Specify structure]
```

**Example:**
```
[ROLE] You are a senior financial analyst with 15 years in SaaS companies.

[ACTION] Analyze this company's Q3 financials and provide investment recommendation.

[CONTEXT] 
- Pre-IPO SaaS startup, B2B market
- $50M ARR, 120% NRR
- Burn rate: $3M/month
- 18 months runway
- Competing for Series C funding

[EXPECTATION]
Provide:
1. Financial health score (1-10)
2. Key metrics analysis (CAC, LTV, Rule of 40)
3. Risk assessment (top 5 risks)
4. Investment recommendation (Yes/No/Maybe)
5. Due diligence checklist

Format: Executive brief (2 pages)
```

### 2. CRAFT Framework (Context, Role, Action, Format, Tone)

```markdown
[CONTEXT]
Situation: [What's happening]
Goal: [What you want to achieve]
Audience: [Who will use/read this]
Constraints: [Limitations or requirements]

[ROLE]

Known for [expertise/approach]
Your style: [characteristics]

[ACTION]
[Specific task with clear verb]
Include: [Must-have elements]
Avoid: [What not to do]

[FORMAT]
Structure: [Layout/organization]
Length: [Word count or pages]
Sections: [Required components]
Visual: [Charts/tables if needed]

[TONE]
Voice: [Formal/casual/technical/etc.]
Personality: [Adjectives describing style]
Audience level: [Expert/beginner/mixed]
```

### 3. Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Prompting

```markdown
Let's solve this step-by-step:

Problem: [Complex problem statement]

Step 1: Understand the problem
- What are we trying to solve?
- What information do we have?
- What information is missing?
- What are the constraints?

Step 2: Break down into sub-problems
- [Sub-problem 1]
- [Sub-problem 2]
- [Sub-problem 3]

Step 3: Solve each sub-problem
For [Sub-problem 1]:
- Approach: [methodology]
- Reasoning: [why this approach]
- Solution: [answer]
- Verification: [how we know it's correct]

[Repeat for each sub-problem]
```
